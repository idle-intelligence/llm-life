## Summary

- a-norules: IoU 1.000 on every generation, every seed, at 16² and 32² — the per-cell LLM reproduces Life exactly on real grids, with no rules in the prompt.
- a-rules: IoU 0.91–1.00 at 16², 0.95–0.99 at 32²; live recall ≥ 0.95. The rules-in-prompt adapter is slightly worse, consistent with its 509/512 lookup score.
- Wall time (from the unit's stamps, not per-generation timers): 15 generations of 16² in ~1 min, 15 of 32² in ~2 min → about 8 s per 1024-cell generation on the 3080 through the f32 adapter path (batched packed prompts). This is the "LLM per pixel" rung as measured natively; the browser rungs come from the merged GGUF.
